# Customization and monetization UI

The Unity flow keeps collection and care expressive while preserving fair ranked races. The home screen links to the stable, scout, wardrobe, course looks, care, room conditions, shop, and season pass.

## Rarity

Rarity describes collection identity and presentation, not competitive strength:

| Tier | UI label | Typical use |
|---|---|---|
| N | Common | Starter silks, basic stable goods, routine feed |
| R | Select | Earned tack, care-kit variants, reward track items |
| SR | Refined | Signature wear, course scenery, rare earned partners |
| SSR | Featured | Season-pass looks, ornate stable decor, premium visual effects |

Cosmetics and care decor do not raise ranked attributes. Paid scout remains paused until the operator supplies the full item pool, each individual probability, featured rates, pity behavior, and duplicate conversion rules. The first free tutorial draw is a local fixed preview and runs once per install.

## Weather, ground, and care

The conditions panel previews sunny, cloudy, rain, and fog; it also saves a ground condition, temperature, and wind for a private-room preset. Ranked events always set the same official conditions for every entrant. The private-room backend and actual weather simulation are not connected in this client preview.

Daily care offers grain and apple, hay and carrot, or fresh water as free choices. A premium stable bowl is direct-purchase decor and does not change the feed effect. Care choice does not change ranked attributes in this client. This keeps paid care from becoming a combat stat or consumable advantage.

## Store and purchase review

The coin pack screen uses the eight draft packs in the planning document and separates paid from free balances. The season pass is shown at ¥1,000 per 30 days with a free and premium reward lane and an immediate outfit reward. Direct cosmetic prices not set in the planning document are marked as proposals.

The purchase-review screen is an age-check design preview. It does not collect a birth date, store age data, or call platform billing. Checkout remains disabled until age-based monthly caps, guardian-consent rules, platform products, tax-inclusive localized prices, and billing callbacks are configured.
